PetBield

Smart ID Cards That Bring Lost Pets Home

PetBield is a QR-code ID card for your pet's collar. If your pet ever wanders off, anyone who finds them can scan the card, share their location, and reach you instantly.

🐾 Found a Pet?

If you've found a pet wearing a PetBield card, scan the QR code on their tag to notify the owner immediately.

How PetBield Works

1

Owner activates the card

Pet owners buy a PetBield card, then use our app to link it to their pet's name, their WhatsApp number, and a preferred drop-off location.

2

Card goes on the pet's collar

The activated card hangs on the pet's neck belt, ready at all times.

3

A stranger finds your pet

They scan the QR code and land on a page showing your pet's name and your instructions.

4

You get notified instantly

Their location and voice message is sent straight to your WhatsApp, so you can go get your pet right away.

Why PetBield?

  • ✅ Instant WhatsApp alert with live location
  • ✅ Voice message from the finder
  • ✅ Your preferred drop-off address shown to the finder
  • ✅ Secure — only you can update your card's info
